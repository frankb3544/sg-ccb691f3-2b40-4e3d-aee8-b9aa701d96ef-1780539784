# AI Growth Comm

## Vision
AI Growth Comm is a community platform empowering realtors and small business owners to discover, learn, and master AI tools. It combines a curated marketplace of one-time purchase AI tools, a comprehensive learning portal with courses and guides, and a vibrant resource hub for live events and user-generated content. The platform offers a Free plan (1 tool access) and a Pro plan (all 4 tools - monthly subscription).

**Target Users**: Realtors, small business owners seeking AI adoption without intimidation.
**Core Value**: Practical AI education + vetted tools + supportive community.

## Design
- **Primary**: `--primary: 245 60% 50%` (deep indigo - tech authority)
- **Accent**: `--accent: 180 70% 45%` (vibrant teal - innovation)
- **Destructive** (celebration highlight): `--destructive: 15 85% 60%` (warm coral)
- **Background**: `--background: 40 25% 96%` (soft cream)
- **Foreground**: `--foreground: 245 25% 15%` (deep slate)
- **Muted**: `--muted: 245 15% 90%` (light indigo-gray)
- **Card**: `--card: 0 0% 100%` (pure white)
- **Heading font**: Clash Display (600, 700 weights)
- **Body font**: Inter (400, 600 weights)
- **Style direction**: Modern gradient cards, floating depth, circular progress indicators, asymmetric layouts, warm community feel balanced with tech sophistication.

## Features
1. **AI Tools Marketplace**: 4 tools (Writrr, Trackrr, Inspirr, Creatrr) with detail pages, demo videos, reviews, success stories. Filterable by category (Realtor/Small Biz).
2. **Learning Portal**: Free & paid courses, guides ("ChatGPT for Beginners"), progress tracking, celebration wins.
3. **Resource Hub**: Live event scheduling (workshops, Q&As), user-generated how-to guides, community interaction.
4. **Subscription Plans**: Free (1 tool) vs Pro (all 4 tools, monthly).
5. **Admin Dashboard**: Full CRUD for tools, courses, events, user content management.
